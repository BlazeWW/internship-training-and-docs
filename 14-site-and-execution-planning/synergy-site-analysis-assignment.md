## Assignment Purpose

The Architectural Draughting students should not begin by drawing final buildings. Their first task is to understand the site and produce a high-level precinct and zoning analysis.

## Required First-Week Outputs

- Site location summary
- Site analysis checklist
- Public/private/restricted zoning proposal
- Vehicle and pedestrian movement notes
- Parking and service access notes
- Noisy versus quiet zone notes
- Security-sensitive zone notes
- First concept board
- Questions for Ben

## Suggested Site Precincts

- Public commercial precinct: restaurants, offices, doctors' rooms
- Automotive/workshop precinct: mechanical workshops, customer drop-off, service routes
- High-security industrial precinct: diamond cutting house, gold refinery, jewellers hub
- Auction house precinct: visitor flow, controlled viewing, event parking
- Logistics/service precinct: deliveries, waste, maintenance, plant/service areas
- Parking and movement precincts
- Buffer/open-space precincts

## Key Planning Questions

- Where are the exact site boundaries?
- Are there existing roads, entrances, or buildings?
- Where should public access be located?
- Should high-security operations have separate access?
- Should the auction house be public-facing or inside a controlled precinct?
- How should workshop noise and traffic be separated from doctors' rooms and restaurants?
- How much parking should be planned initially?
- What must be built first if the project is phased?
