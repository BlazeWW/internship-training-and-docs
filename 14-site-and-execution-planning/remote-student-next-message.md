# Remote Internship: First Week Training and Project Preparation

Dear Interns,

As the internship is remote, we will not rely on one fixed meeting time for everyone. Some students are full-time and others are part-time, so meetings and informal talks will be arranged based on availability.

Your responsibility is to communicate clearly, keep your work organised, and ask questions early.

## What You Need to Do First

Please confirm the following:

- Full name and surname
- CTU group or course
- Year level, where applicable
- Whether you are full-time or part-time
- General availability
- Exam or study commitments
- GitHub username
- Which tools you already have installed
- Any setup problems you are experiencing

## First Week Focus by Group

### Software Engineering 1 and 2

You will begin with GitHub, TypeScript, Next.js, project structure, database thinking, user stories, and the Blaze Diagnostics MVP backlog.

### Software Development 1 and 2

You will begin with GitHub, web fundamentals, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Next.js basics, and small frontend or workflow tasks.

### Cloud Administration

You will begin with repository access, environment variables, deployment concepts, database hosting, backups, monitoring, and CI/CD basics.

### Cyber Security

You will begin with OWASP Top 10, role-based access, customer data protection, GitHub secret handling, and security checklists for both Blaze Diagnostics and Synergy Inc.

### Architectural Draughting

You will begin with the Synergy Inc. site analysis. The property is approximately 24 hectares. Your first task is to understand the site, identify possible zones, study access and movement, and prepare a first concept board.

Site reference:

https://maps.app.goo.gl/27Jewne9cvYxC4SW8

## Important

Do not worry if you do not understand everything immediately. The first week is for training, setup, questions, and preparation.

Progress is expected. Perfection is not expected.
