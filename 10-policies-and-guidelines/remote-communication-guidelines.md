# Remote Communication Guidelines

## Remote Internship Expectations

Because this internship is remote, communication must be clear and proactive.

Students should communicate:

- What they are working on
- What they completed
- What they are struggling with
- When they are available
- When they are unavailable
- What help they need

## Good Update Example

```text
Today I worked through the GitHub basics and started learning TypeScript. I understand commits and branches better now, but I still need help with pull requests. I am available tomorrow after 15:00.
```

## Poor Update Example

```text
I am done.
```

A good update gives enough information for someone else to understand your progress.
