# Synergy Inc. Project Brief

## Project Overview

Synergy Inc. is an architectural and business development project involving a multi-purpose business centre.

The property may include:

- Offices
- Restaurants
- Doctors' rooms
- Mechanical workshops
- Diamond cutting house
- Gold refinery
- Jewellers hub
- Auction house

## CAD Focus

CAD students will support:

- Research
- Space planning
- Zoning concepts
- Conceptual layouts
- Movement-flow diagrams
- Public/private/restricted area separation
- Exported drawings for review
- Revision logs
- Final concept deliverables

## Security Considerations

Some spaces require stronger planning around access control and separation from public areas, especially:

- Diamond cutting house
- Gold refinery
- Jewellers hub
- Auction house handling and storage zones
