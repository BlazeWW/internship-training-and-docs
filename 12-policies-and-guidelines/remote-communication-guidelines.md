# Remote Communication Guidelines

This internship is remote, and meetings are arranged informally based on availability.

## Communication Expectations

Students must communicate clearly about:

- Availability
- Exam or study commitments
- Progress
- Blockers
- Questions
- Completed work
- Links to pull requests, documents, or exports

## Good Update Example

```text
Today I worked through the GitHub workflow and started reviewing the customer feature. I understand branches and commits better, but I still need help with pull requests. I am available tomorrow after 15:00.
```

## When To Ask For Help

Ask for help when:

- You do not understand the task
- You cannot set up a tool
- You are blocked for more than a reasonable amount of time
- You are unsure whether your work is correct
- You will not be available for an agreed discussion

Do not wait until the deadline to say you are blocked.
