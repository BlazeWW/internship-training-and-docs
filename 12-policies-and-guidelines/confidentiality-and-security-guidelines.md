# Confidentiality and Security Guidelines

Students may be exposed to project ideas, business processes, software architecture, and internal planning documents.

## General Rules

- Do not share project information outside the internship group.
- Do not share repository access with anyone else.
- Do not share screenshots, source files, concepts, or planning documents publicly.
- Do not commit passwords, API keys, access tokens, `.env` files, private URLs, or database credentials.
- Do not work on production systems unless specifically instructed.
- Report mistakes early.

## Software and Cloud Rules

- Use placeholder values in examples.
- Keep `.env` files out of GitHub.
- Do not expose customer data.
- Do not upload private keys or credentials.
- Ask before changing infrastructure or deployment settings.

## Cyber Security Rules

- Only test approved environments.
- Do not attempt unauthorized access.
- Document findings responsibly.
- Focus on improving security, not proving a point.

## Architectural Draughting Rules

- Do not upload confidential site information unless approved.
- Keep files named and versioned correctly.
- Use exported review copies instead of unnecessary large source files.
- Do not delete or overwrite another student's work.
